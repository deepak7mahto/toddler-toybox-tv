# Privacy Policy — Toddler Toybox

**Effective Date:** November 26, 2026  
**Publisher:** Trishud Enterprises

## Summary
**Toddler Toybox** is a 100% offline, ad-free play app designed for young children (ages 2–4) on Android TV, Android phones, and tablets.

## Data Collection
We collect **zero** personal or non-personal data. Specifically:
- **No Internet Access:** The app does not request the `INTERNET` permission and cannot transmit or receive data over any network.
- **No Accounts or Logins:** No registration, email, name, or profile information is collected.
- **No Analytics or Ads:** The app contains no advertising SDKs, third-party trackers, crash reporters, or in-app purchases.
- **No Device Permissions:** The app does not request access to the camera, microphone, location, contacts, or external storage.
- **On-Device Audio Only:** All graphics are rendered locally, all sound effects are stored/generated on-device, and spoken words use Android's built-in on-device Text-to-Speech engine.

## Children's Privacy (Google Play Families Policy & COPPA)
Because Toddler Toybox does not collect, store, or share any information from any user—including children under 13—it complies fully with Google Play's Families Policy and COPPA.
